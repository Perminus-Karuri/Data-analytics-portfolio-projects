**2. Key findings, insights and recommendation**

**Sales analysis**
By category: Technology category generated most sales worth $835,900.14, Furniture category was second with $733,047.06 and Office supplies category with $703,502.87.
By region: West region recorded the highest sales among the 4 regions ($713,471.45), East region with sales worth $672,194.15, Central region with sales worth $497,800.88 while the South region had the lowest sales ($388,983.59).
By segment: Consumer segment had the most sales worth $1,150,166.18, Corporate segment with sales worth $696,604.60 and Home office with $425,679.29.

**Profitability analysis**
By category: Technology recorded highest profit of $145,387.81, Office supplies with profits worth $120,489.93 and Furniture with the lowest profits of $16,980.72. Profit margin for each category was as follows: Technology - 17.39%, Office supplies - 17.13% and Furniture - 2.32%.
By region: West region had the highest profit amounting to $106,021.47, East region with $90,672.04, South region with $46,036 and Central region with the lowest profit among the 4 regions of $40,128.95. Profit margins were as follows: West - 14.86%, East - 13.49%, South - 11.83% and Central - 8.06%.
By segment: Consumer segment had the highest profit worth $132,669.18, Corporate segment with $90,366.57 and Home office segment with $59,822.01. However, in terms of profit margins, Home office has the highest profit margin with 14.05%, Corporate with 12.97% and Consumer segment with 11.53%. Consumer segment is the largest segment by sales and absolute profit but it is the lowest in terms of profit margin of the three segments. Home Office segment generates the highest profit margin despite having the lowest sales and absolute profit. Consumer has greater sales volume but converts a smaller portion of its revenue into profit while the Home Office segment has stronger profitability relative to its sales.

**Discount analysis**
Highest discount is 80% and the lowest being 0%. This then helps create groups of discount bands from 0% - 10% through 71% - 80%.
By segment: Across the three segments, profits remained positive from 0% through 11% - 20% discount bands. However, profitability became negative from 21% - 30% and remained negative across the highest discount bands through 71% - 80%. Discounts above 20% are associated with negative profitability.
By category: In the Furniture category, profits remained positive from 0% through to the 11% - 20% discount band. However, profitability became negative from the 21% - 30% discount band onwards. In the Office Supplies category, profits remained positive from 0% through to the 11% - 20% discount band, while discounts above 40% were associated with negative profitability. In the Technology category, profits remained positive across the 0% to 21% - 30% discount bands. Negative profitability was realized across the 31% - 40% discount band and higher discount levels. High discounting is associated with negative profitability.

**Recommendations**
From the above analysis, discounts above 20% are associated with negative profitability. Review and tighten discounting policies above 20%, particularly for segments and categories that are associated with negative profitability.
